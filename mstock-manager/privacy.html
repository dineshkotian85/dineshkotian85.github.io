Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application mStock Manager ("Application") available on the Google Play Store. The software environment has been built and is maintained by Dinesh Keshav Kotian as an ad-supported engine.

1. Data Collection Architecture & Usage

  • Locally Encrypted Databases: Features requiring data configurations (such as custom stock entries, portfolio values, or logs) process information entirely on your internal storage device hardware. We do not transmit or log this data to external server nodes.
  • Automated Telemetry: Basic system data—such as operating system details, device type identifiers, and crash timestamps—are logged dynamically to prevent software regression.

2. Integrated Advertising Partners

This environment coordinates advertising parameters through Google AdMob. AdMob deploys programmatic crawlers to parse dynamic identifiers on your device, enabling relevant ad layouts. Review how Google protects data here:

  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy Hub

3. Security & Framework Adaptations

Reasonable standard cryptographic methods are deployed across core updates to preserve application integrity. This structure strictly aligns with developer rules for minors. We do not intentionally track metrics for anyone under 13.
