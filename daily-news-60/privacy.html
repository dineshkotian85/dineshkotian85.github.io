Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Daily News 60 ("Application") available on the Google Play Store. The software environment has been built and is maintained by Dinesh Keshav Kotian as an ad-supported engine.

1. Data Pipelines

Our infrastructure fetches curated news summaries from public syndications. The system caches textual summaries locally on your device storage to minimize latency. We do not construct structural reading histories tied to personal biometric details or accounts.
