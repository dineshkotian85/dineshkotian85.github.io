Privacy Policy for Music Video Player

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Music Video Player ("the App") is a media player application that lets you browse and play music and video files available on your Android device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

In short: The App needs access to media stored on your device so that it can find and play your music and videos. The App does not require you to create an account. Advertising and consent services may process information as described below.

1. Information the App Accesses

Media files on your device

With your permission, the App accesses music and video files stored on your Android device. This includes media metadata such as title, artist, album, duration, file size, date added, and other metadata supplied by Android's MediaStore.

This access is used to build your local music/video library, display media information, organize media, and play the selected files.

Device permissions

The App may request Android permissions for:

  • Reading audio files.
  • Reading video files.
  • Notifications, where required for playback notifications.
  • Foreground media playback, where required by Android for ongoing playback.

You can manage applicable permissions through Android system settings. If media access is denied, some or all playback and library features may not work.

2. How Your Media Is Used

The App uses media information primarily to provide its local playback and library features. Media selection and playback are controlled by you.

Based on the current App implementation, your personal music and video files are accessed through Android MediaStore and are not uploaded by the App to a developer-operated server for storage or account management.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on the applicable Google services, your device settings, and consent choices.

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent where required. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be shown a consent or privacy choices form before personalized advertising is enabled.

Google may use information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and related services according to Google's own policies.

For more information, please review Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

4. No User Account

The App does not require you to register for or maintain a user account to use its core local music and video playback functionality.

5. Data Storage

The App may maintain application data and preferences on your device to support its functionality. Media files remain under the control of the Android operating system and are not copied to a developer-operated account as part of the App's normal local playback functionality.

6. Data Sharing

The App does not sell your personal information. Information processed by third-party advertising or consent services may be handled by those providers according to their respective privacy policies and your consent choices.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for account creation.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the App and its handling of information in a manner intended to protect user privacy. However, no application, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

  • You can grant or deny media permissions through Android.
  • You can manage notification permissions through Android.
  • You can manage advertising/privacy choices when the applicable consent form is presented.
  • You can reset or remove App data through Android settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Music Video Player, please use the developer contact information provided on the App's Google Play Store listing.

12. Third-Party Services

The App currently uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent management. These services are operated by Google and are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.
